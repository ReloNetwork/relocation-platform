## Video Production Notes

### Camera Setup
- **Lighting:** Natural light from window or ring light for professional appearance
- **Background:** Clean, minimal background (bookshelf, plain wall, or office setup)
- **Camera position:** Eye level, arms length distance for talking head segments

### Screen Recording
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum for Stripe dashboard and platform demos
- **Cursor highlighting:** Use tools like Screenflick (Mac) or OBS to highlight cursor
- **Zoom technique:** Zoom in on important numbers and interface elements

### Editing Guidelines
- **Pacing:** Cut out pauses longer than 2 seconds
- **Graphics:** Add text overlays for key statistics and main points
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts, avoid flashy transitions that distract from content
- **Music:** Light background music during intro/outro only, silent during main content

### Thumbnail Design
- **Main element:** Your face + Stripe dashboard showing revenue
- **Text overlay:** "£180K Without Coding" in large, bold font
- **Colors:** Navy background with gold accents matching your brand
- **Emotions:** Confident, successful expression without being overly salesy
