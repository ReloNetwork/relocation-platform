Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our executive relocation service.

Why 7–30 days vs. 60–120 days DIY?

DIY relocations take months because you're navigating unfamiliar systems, property markets, and school applications alone. We've pre-mapped London's executive housing market, have relationships with top agents, and know which schools accept mid-year transfers. Our 72-hour audit eliminates the research phase that typically takes weeks.

Who is Relo Network for?

Senior executives, C-suite leaders, and high-net-worth individuals relocating to London for work. We also serve corporate HR teams managing multiple executive relocations. If you're earning £150K+ and time is more valuable than money, we're for you.

What's included in the 72-Hour Audit?

Area fit analysis based on your commute and lifestyle preferences, curated property shortlist with viewing itinerary optimization, school catchment area mapping for your children's ages, and tenancy agreement review with executive-specific clauses. You get a complete relocation roadmap, not just property listings.

How do milestone guarantees work?

If we miss our Day 7, 14, or 30 deliverable commitments, you receive a 10% service credit automatically. No arguments, no excuses. We track every milestone and send you progress updates. Our reputation depends on hitting these deadlines because executive moves can't afford delays.

Do you handle visas and immigration?

We coordinate with your immigration lawyers but don't provide legal advice. We know which neighborhoods work best for different visa types and can recommend trusted immigration specialists. Think of us as the lifestyle and logistics experts while your lawyers handle the paperwork.

What if my employee declines the relocation?

Our 72-hour audit often changes minds by removing the unknowns that create anxiety. We show them exactly where they'll live, which schools their kids will attend, and how their daily life will work. Most declines happen due to fear of the unknown, not the actual move.
