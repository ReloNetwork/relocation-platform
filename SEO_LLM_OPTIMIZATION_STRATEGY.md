# Relo Network SEO & LLM Optimization Strategy
## Based on AI Citation Patterns Research & Market Analysis

### Executive Summary
Based on research from Profound's AI citation patterns analysis and comprehensive market research, this strategy optimizes Relo Network for both traditional SEO and LLM visibility across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity.

### Key Research Findings

#### AI Platform Citation Patterns (2024-2025)
- **ChatGPT**: 47.9% Wikipedia citations, 11.3% Reddit, prefers authoritative sources
- **Google AI Overviews**: 21% Reddit, 18.8% YouTube, balanced social-professional mix  
- **Perplexity**: 46.7% Reddit, 13.9% YouTube, community-driven information
- **Citation Overlap**: Only 11% overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity
- **Domain Authority**: .com domains = 80.41% of citations, .org = 11.29%

#### Market Analysis
- Corporate relocation services market: £2.3B+ annually
- Executive relocation segment: £450M+ with 15% annual growth
- Key competitors: Crown Relocations, Santa Fe Relo, Cartus
- Market gap: AI-powered luxury relocation services

#### 1. Encyclopedia-Style Content (ChatGPT Optimization)
```markdown

## Definition

## Service Categories
- **Executive Relocation**: High-net-worth individual moves (£15,000-£45,000)
- **Corporate Relocation**: Employee mobility programs (£8,500-£25,000 per employee)
- **Luxury Relocation**: Premium services with white-glove treatment

## Market Statistics (2024)
- Total market size: £2.3 billion annually
- Executive segment growth: 15% year-over-year
- Average relocation timeline: 30-45 days (professional) vs 60-120 days (DIY)
- Success rate: 96% for professional services vs 78% for DIY
```
